# Definition : threat 
 A threat is any person, event, or circumstance with the potential to harm your systems, data, or operations.
 # Vulnerability: 
 A vulnerability is a weakness or flaw in your systems, software, processes, or people that a threat could exploit. 
 # Attack:
 An attack is the actual act of exploiting a vulnerability to cause harm. 
 # Explain : Phishing
 Phishing is a social engineering attack where cybercriminals send fraudulent emails, texts, or messages disguised as trusted sources to trick you into revealing sensitive data or clicking malicious links.
 # Malware :
 Malware  is any code intentionally designed to damage, disrupt, or gain unauthorized access to systems 
 # Ransomware :
 Ransomware is a type of malware that encrypts your files and holds them hostage until you pay a ransom for the decryption key. 
 # Social engineering :
 Social engineering is the psychological manipulation of people into divulging confidential information or performing actions that compromise security.
# Password attack :
A password attack is any attempt to gain unauthorized access by cracking, guessing, or stealing a user's password. 
# DoS/DDoS:
 A DoS (Denial of Service) floods a system with traffic to crash it, a DDoS (Distributed) does the same but uses thousands of hijacked devices  globally, making it nearly impossible to block by just filtering one source.

The 2020 Twitter Bitcoin scam – attackers called Twitter employees pretending to be IT support, tricked them into giving admin credentials, and took over high-profile accounts to post crypto scams.
# Password Attack: 
The 2019 "Collection #1" breach – hackers used a massive dump of 773 million stolen passwords to perform credential stuffing against millions of Spotify and Netflix accounts, hijacking them for resale.
# DoS/DDoS: 
The 2016 Mirai botnet attack on Dyn DNS – hacked IoT devices (cameras, routers) flooded Dyn with traffic, taking down major sites like Twitter, Netflix, and Reddit across the entire US East Coast.
